# Planted Contradictions Documentation
**Rulebook RAG — Problem #1: "The Rulebook That Argues With Itself"**

This document records the exact locations, clause numbers, document sources, and conflicting statements for the 3 deliberate contradictions planted within the university academic regulations corpus.

## Contradiction #1: Final Examination Attendance Qualification

### Description
The regulations contain two directly opposing rules regarding the mandatory attendance percentage required for a student to take final semester examinations when a valid medical certificate is provided.

### Conflicting Passages & Sources

1. **Strict 75% Mandatory Attendance Rule**:
   - **Source Document**: `corpus/academic_regulations.md`
   - **Sections**: Chapter 3 (Section 3.1, Clause 3.1.a) & Chapter 11 (Section 11.2, Clause 11.2.a)
   - **Text**: 
     > *"Clause 3.1.a: A student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course across the semester to be eligible to sit for the end-semester final examination in that course."*  
     > *"Clause 11.2.a: Under no circumstances shall any Dean waiver, departmental committee exception, or medical exemption certificate lower the mandatory final examination attendance threshold below 75% for any course or student."*

2. **60% Medical Exemption Waiver Rule**:
   - **Source Document**: `corpus/examination_and_records_policy.pdf`
   - **Section**: Section 4 (Medical Exemptions for Final Examinations, Section 4.2)
   - **Text**: 
     > *"4.2 Medical Attendance Exemption Policy: Students who submit a valid, verified medical certificate from a university-approved hospital within 5 working days are granted an official attendance waiver, permitting them to sit for the final examination provided their recorded attendance is at least 60%."*

## Contradiction #2: Late Tuition Fee Payment Penalty & Grace Period

### Description
The regulations conflict on whether a student paying tuition fees after the official due date is granted a 7-day penalty-free grace period or is immediately charged a $50 late payment fee on day 1.

### Conflicting Passages & Sources

1. **7-Day Grace Period Policy**:
   - **Source Document**: `corpus/academic_regulations.md`
   - **Section**: Chapter 4 (Tuition Fees, Schedules and Financial Policies, Section 4.2, Clause 4.2.a)
   - **Text**: 
     > *"Clause 4.2.a (Grace Period): Students paying tuition fees after the official due date are granted a 7-day grace period (calendar days 1 through 7 post-due date) during which no late payment penalty fee shall be levied or accrued on the account."*

2. **Immediate $50 Fee / Zero Grace Period Policy**:
   - **Source Document**: `corpus/academic_regulations.md` & `corpus/examination_and_records_policy.pdf`
   - **Sections**: `academic_regulations.md` (Section 4.5, Clause 4.5.a) & `examination_and_records_policy.pdf` (Section 5.1)
   - **Text**: 
     > *"Clause 4.5.a: A mandatory late payment penalty fee of $50 per week is applied immediately starting on the first calendar day following the published payment due date, with zero grace period allowed for late payments."*  
     > *"Section 5.1: A mandatory late payment penalty fee of $50 per week applies immediately starting the day following the payment due date, with no grace period applicable under any circumstances."*

## Contradiction #3: Course Withdrawal Transcript Record & Notation

### Description
The policies disagree on whether a course dropped after Week 4 appears on the official academic transcript with a grade of 'W' or can be completely erased without transcript record up to Week 8.

### Conflicting Passages & Sources

1. **Week 8 Clean Transcript Erasure Rule**:
   - **Source Document**: `corpus/academic_regulations.md`
   - **Section**: Chapter 5 (Course Withdrawal and Add/Drop Procedures, Section 5.3, Clause 5.3.a)
   - **Text**: 
     > *"Clause 5.3.a: A student may formally withdraw from any registered course up to the end of Week 8 of the semester without incurring any academic penalty, and the withdrawn course shall be completely erased from the student's official transcript record."*

2. **Week 4 Mandatory Transcript 'W' Notation Rule**:
   - **Source Document**: `corpus/examination_and_records_policy.pdf`
   - **Section**: Section 6 (Transcript Record of Course Withdrawals, Section 6.1)
   - **Text**: 
     > *"Section 6.1: Any course from which a student withdraws after Week 4 of the semester must remain permanently listed on the official academic transcript with a recorded grade of 'W' (Withdrawn). No course dropped after Week 4 may be erased from the transcript."*
